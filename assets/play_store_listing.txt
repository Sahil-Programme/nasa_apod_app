Short Description (<=80)
Explore NASA's APOD with slideshows, videos, and one-tap wallpapers.

Full Description (<=4000)
Stellar Lens helps you discover space every day through NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD).

Browse today's featured image, jump to any date, or explore random APOD entries to find incredible photos, deep-sky captures, and fascinating astronomy stories. Each entry includes the original NASA explanation so you can learn the science behind what you see.

Key features:
- Daily APOD discovery with date picker and random mode
- Immersive slideshow mode for uninterrupted space viewing
- Inline media support for APOD videos and mixed media posts
- Wallpaper tools to set your favorite APOD as your device wallpaper
- Built-in image framing flow to zoom and position wallpapers before applying
- Cache support for smoother browsing and quicker reloads
- Low Internet Usage mode for lighter image loading

Stellar Lens is designed for people who love astronomy, photography, and learning something new about the universe in just a few minutes each day.

Note:
Stellar Lens is an independent app that uses NASA's public APOD API.
